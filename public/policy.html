Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Cyber Empire (“Antaqor”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Antaqor website, mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the “Service”). By using the Service you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information collected automatically when you use the Service, and information received from third parties such as identity providers.

  • Account information: name, email address, profile photo, username, and password (hashed).
  • Content you create: posts, comments, reactions, messages, uploads, and AI prompts.
  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, pages viewed, referring URL, and timestamps.
  • Payment information: processed by our payment partners; we do not store full card numbers.

2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, measure traffic, and improve the Service. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the Service may not function correctly without them.

3. Analytics

We use analytics tools (such as web analytics providers and server logs) to understand how the Service is used so we can improve performance, reliability, and user experience. These tools may collect information such as the pages you visit, time on page, and approximate location derived from IP.

4. Login and Authentication

You can create an account using your email and password or by signing in with a third-party identity provider, including Facebook Login. When you sign in with a third-party provider, we receive only the data you authorize that provider to share with us.

5. Facebook Login and Meta Platform APIs

Antaqor uses Facebook Login and the Meta Platform APIs to enable account creation and authentication. When you choose to sign in with Facebook, we request the following permissions:

  • public_profile — your Facebook name, profile picture, and public user ID.
  • email — the primary email address associated with your Facebook account.

We use this information solely to create and maintain your Antaqor account, identify you across sessions, personalize your experience, and contact you about the Service. We do not post to your Facebook account, access your friends list, or share your Facebook data with advertisers.

You can revoke Antaqor’s access to your Facebook account at any time at facebook.com/settings → Apps and Websites. For a full deletion of your Antaqor data, see our Data Deletion page.

6. Third-Party APIs and Services

We integrate third-party services to provide certain features, including authentication, payments, hosting, email delivery, push notifications, and AI model providers. These services receive only the data needed to perform their function and are bound by their own privacy policies and our data-processing agreements.

7. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To authenticate users and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • To personalize content and recommendations.
  • To send transactional and service-related communications.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

8. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers operated by us and by reputable cloud infrastructure providers. We retain personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

9. Data Security

We use industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), password hashing, access controls, and regular security reviews. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights

You may access, update, or delete information in your account at any time from your account settings. You may also contact us at antaqor@antaqor.com to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for processing where consent is the legal basis.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.

11. GDPR Rights (European Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of access, rectification, and erasure.
  • Right to restrict or object to processing.
  • Right to data portability.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time.
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Our legal bases for processing include consent, contract performance, legitimate interests, and compliance with legal obligations.

12. CCPA Rights (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • Right to delete personal information we collect from you.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell your personal information).
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

13. International Users

Antaqor is operated from Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia. If you access the Service from outside Mongolia, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries with different data-protection laws than your own. By using the Service, you consent to such transfers.

14. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.
