Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: May 15, 2026

Antaqor (operated by Cyber Empire) lets you sign in with Facebook Login. In accordance with the Meta Platform Terms and applicable data-protection laws, this page explains how to remove our app’s access to your Facebook account and how to request permanent deletion of the personal data we hold about you.

Why this page exists

If you used “Continue with Facebook” to access Antaqor, Meta requires us to provide clear instructions for revoking app permissions and deleting any user data we received through Facebook Login. This page satisfies that requirement and also serves users who signed up by email or other methods.

Option 1 — Remove Antaqor’s access from Facebook

Step 1Open Facebook Settings

  1. Log in to your Facebook account.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites, or open facebook.com/settings?tab=applications directly.
  3. Find Antaqor in the list of active apps.
  4. Click Remove and confirm.

This revokes Antaqor’s ability to access your Facebook profile going forward. To also delete the data we already received, complete Option 2 below.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Step 2Email us at antaqor@antaqor.com

To permanently delete your Antaqor account and all associated personal data, send an email to antaqor@antaqor.com from the email address registered to your Antaqor account, with the subject line:

“Data Deletion Request”

Please include in the body of the email:

  • Your registered email address or Facebook user ID.
  • Your Antaqor username (if known).
  • A short confirmation that you are requesting permanent deletion of your account and data.

What data is deleted

Once we verify your request, we permanently delete:

  • Your account record, profile information, and authentication tokens.
  • Personal data received from Facebook Login (name, email, profile picture, Facebook user ID).
  • User-generated content (posts, comments, reactions, messages, uploads) tied to your account.
  • AI conversation history and per-user memory.
  • Usage logs and analytics identifiers associated with your account.
Please note: we may retain a minimal record (e.g., the fact that a deletion occurred, anonymized transaction or fraud-prevention data) for the limited period required by law, tax, accounting, or legitimate security purposes. Such records do not contain information that can be used to re-identify you.

Processing timeline

We process verified deletion requests within 30 days of receipt. Most requests are completed within 7 business days. Backups containing your data are rotated out within an additional 30 days and are isolated from active systems in the meantime.

Confirmation

Once your data has been deleted, we will send a confirmation email to the address used to submit the request (or the address registered to the account). If you do not receive confirmation within 30 days, please re-send your request and we will investigate.

Children and parental requests

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has created an account or that we hold a child’s personal information, contact us using the details below and we will delete it promptly.
